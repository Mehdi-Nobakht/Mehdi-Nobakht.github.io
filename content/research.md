My research sits at the intersection of **distributed systems** and **systems security**. On the systems side, I work on software-defined networking (SDN), time-sensitive networking (TSN), programmable networking and cyber-physical systems. On the security side, I work on network and IoT security, ML-driven anomaly and intrusion detection, and cyber-physical systems security.

Recent work includes integrating wired and wireless TSN with SDN, priority-based scheduling for consistent network updates in SDN, and detecting DDoS flooding attacks with machine learning on programmable switches. Earlier work looked at securing smart-home IoT networks with SDN and OpenFlow (IoT-IDM, IoT-NetSec) and static permission analysis of apps in IoT programming frameworks (PGFit).
