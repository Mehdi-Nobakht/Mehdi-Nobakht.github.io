I am an education-focused academic at the University of Sydney, teaching computer science in the Faculty of Engineering and information systems and business analytics in the Business School. Since 2024, I have coordinated and taught foundation, undergraduate and postgraduate courses there. My research background is in computer networking and cybersecurity, with a particular interest in network analytics, programmable and software-defined networking (SDN), time-sensitive networking (TSN), and cyber-physical and IoT security.

Before 2024, I was a Research Fellow (2021–2023) and Associate Lecturer (2019–2021) at the [School of Systems and Computing](https://www.unsw.edu.au/canberra/about-us/our-schools/school-of-systems-and-computing) (formerly SEIT), UNSW Canberra, where I taught cybersecurity and worked on SDN for time-sensitive distributed systems. I completed my Ph.D. in Computer Science at [UNSW Sydney](https://www.unsw.edu.au/engineering/our-schools/computer-science-and-engineering) in 2018, with a thesis on *The Internet of Things: Securing Devices and User Data*. Earlier, I received my M.Sc. (Tech) in Information Technology from the [University of Turku](http://www.utu.fi/en/), Finland in 2013, after working as a research assistant at [Åbo Akademi University](https://www.abo.fi/en/). Before moving into research, I spent over a decade in industry, working in telecommunications software engineering.
